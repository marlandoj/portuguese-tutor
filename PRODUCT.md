# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent English-speaking learners practicing European Portuguese in short, repeated sessions on desktop or mobile. They arrive to continue a lesson, rehearse pronunciation, review vocabulary, or speak with an AI coach. The interface must orient them immediately while making the learning environment feel connected to Portugal.

## Product Purpose

Português Tutor combines a structured three-level curriculum, listening, pronunciation, missions, progress tracking, and AI conversation in one private-progress learning tool. Success means learners return frequently, recognize European Portuguese in context, and build confidence speaking without relearning the interface.

## Brand Personality

Open, cultivated, and sunlit. The experience should feel distinctly Portuguese and lived-in, balancing Atlantic energy with the patient craft of daily study.

## Anti-references

- Generic green-and-red flag palettes
- Brazilian cultural or geographic imagery
- Tourism collages, souvenir graphics, and postcard pastiche
- Blue-only dashboards that treat azulejos as a generic pattern
- Decorative Mediterranean styling disconnected from Portugal
- Cinematic effects that delay navigation or obscure learning content

## Design Principles

1. Practice stays primary. Cultural atmosphere frames the task but never competes with it.
2. One structure, three worlds. Navigation and controls remain stable while material, imagery, and mood change substantially.
3. Portugal is specific. Lisbon light, Atlantic geography, craft materials, and travel details replace generic Iberian shorthand.
4. Progress should feel tangible. Lessons, streaks, speech scores, and missions remain easy to scan in every theme.
5. Delight is earned through transitions, texture, and small responsive details rather than extra UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Theme selection must have a visible label, focus treatment, and full keyboard support. Decorative motion is disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`; backgrounds retain a readable static treatment. Portuguese diacritics must remain clear at all sizes, and color is never the sole state indicator.
